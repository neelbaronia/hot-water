Where does hot water come from?
Domestic hot water
Section A–A · not to scale

Where it comes from

A water heater: an insulated tank in the basement, garage or closet.

How it stays hot

Foam insulation, plus a thermostat that reheats it whenever it cools.

How it gets up the pipe

City water pressure pushes cold in at the bottom, and hot out the top.

Tankless heaters skip the tank and heat the water as it flows through. Same pressure, same pipes.
